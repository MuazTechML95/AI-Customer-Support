# NovaCart — Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty Coverage
Most electronics sold on NovaCart include a **12-month manufacturer warranty** covering manufacturing defects, unless otherwise stated on the product page.

## What's Covered
- Hardware malfunctions under normal use
- Manufacturing defects
- Battery defects (if reported within 6 months of purchase)

## What's Not Covered
- Accidental damage (drops, water damage, cracked screens)
- Unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from misuse or third-party accessories

## How to Claim Warranty
1. Contact customer support with your order number and a description of the issue.
2. Provide photos/videos if requested.
3. Approved claims will receive a prepaid shipping label to send the item for inspection.
4. Repair, replacement, or refund will be issued within 10–15 business days of inspection.

## Extended Warranty
An optional Extended Warranty (+12 months) can be purchased at checkout for eligible electronics, covering accidental damage once per policy period.

## Warranty Transferability
Standard warranties are non-transferable and apply only to the original purchaser.
