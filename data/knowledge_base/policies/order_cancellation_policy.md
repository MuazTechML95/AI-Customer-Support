# NovaCart — Order Cancellation Policy

## Cancellation Window
Orders can be cancelled free of charge **within 1 hour** of placing the order, as long as the order has not yet entered processing.

## How to Cancel
Go to "My Orders," select the order, and click "Cancel Order." If the option is unavailable, the order has already moved to processing/shipping.

## After Processing Begins
Once an order has been processed or shipped, it cannot be cancelled. You may instead request a return once delivered, per our Return & Refund Policy.

## Cancellation by NovaCart
NovaCart reserves the right to cancel orders due to:
- Stock unavailability
- Pricing/listing errors
- Suspected fraudulent activity

In such cases, a full refund is issued automatically within 3–5 business days.

## Partial Cancellations
For multi-item orders, individual items may be cancelled separately within the cancellation window if they have not yet shipped.
