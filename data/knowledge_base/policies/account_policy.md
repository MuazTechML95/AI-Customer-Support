# NovaCart — Account Policy

## Account Creation
Users must be at least 18 years old to create a NovaCart account, or have parental/guardian consent if under 18.

## Account Security
Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their password. NovaCart recommends enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) in Account Settings.

## Updating Account Information
You can update your name, email, phone number, and addresses anytime from "Account Settings."

## Password Reset
If you forget your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page to receive a reset link via email, valid for 30 minutes.

## Account Suspension
NovaCart may suspend accounts found violating Terms of Service, including fraudulent activity, abuse of return policies, or payment fraud.

## Account Deletion
Users can request permanent account deletion via Account Settings or by emailing support@novacart.com. Deletion requests are processed within 14 business days, after which order history cannot be recovered.

## Data Privacy
Account data is handled per NovaCart's Privacy Policy and is never sold to third parties.
