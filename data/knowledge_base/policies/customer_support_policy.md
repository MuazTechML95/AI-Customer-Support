# NovaCart — Customer Support Policy

## Support Channels
- Live Chat: Available on website and app (9 AM–9 PM, Mon–Sat)
- Email: support@novacart.com (response within 24 hours)
- Phone: +1-800-555-0199 (9 AM–9 PM, Mon–Sat)

## Response Time Commitments
- Live Chat: Immediate to 5 minutes
- Email: Within 24 business hours
- Phone: Average wait time under 5 minutes during business hours

## Escalation Process
If an issue is not resolved by first-level support, customers can request escalation to a Senior Support Specialist by replying "Escalate" in any support conversation.

## Priority Support
Priority/expedited support is available for orders affected by shipping errors, defective items, or payment disputes.

## What Support Can Help With
- Order status, tracking, and delivery issues
- Returns, refunds, and exchanges
- Payment and billing questions
- Account access issues
- Product information

## What Requires Human Support
Complex disputes, legal inquiries, and cases requiring identity verification are handled exclusively by human support agents and cannot be resolved by the AI assistant.
