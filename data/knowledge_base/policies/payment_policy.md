# NovaCart — Payment Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
- Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- PayPal
- NovaCart Wallet (store credit)
- Apple Pay / Google Pay (on mobile app only)

## Payment Security
All transactions are processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. NovaCart does not store full card numbers on its servers.

## Currency
All prices are listed and charged in USD unless otherwise stated at checkout for supported regions.

## Failed Payments
If a payment fails, the order will not be confirmed. Please verify your card details or try an alternate payment method. Contact your bank if the issue persists.

## Payment Authorization Holds
For certain orders, a temporary authorization hold may appear on your statement before the final charge is confirmed. This is not a duplicate charge.

## Installment Payments
NovaCart supports "Pay in 4" installment plans on orders above $100 through supported third-party providers, shown at checkout when eligible.

## Billing Disputes
For any unrecognized charge, contact support@novacart.com within 60 days of the transaction date.
