# NovaCart — Shipping Policy

## Shipping Coverage
NovaCart currently ships to the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom.

## Shipping Methods & Timeframes
- **Standard Shipping:** 5–7 business days — $4.99 (Free on orders over $50)
- **Express Shipping:** 2–3 business days — $12.99
- **Next-Day Delivery:** 1 business day — $24.99 (available in select cities only)

## Order Processing Time
Orders are processed within 1–2 business days before shipping. Orders placed after 3:00 PM are processed the next business day.

## Shipping Confirmation
You will receive an email and SMS with tracking information once your order ships.

## Delayed or Lost Shipments
If your shipment is delayed beyond the estimated delivery window by more than 3 business days, contact customer support with your order number for investigation.

## Address Accuracy
NovaCart is not responsible for delays or non-delivery caused by incorrect or incomplete shipping addresses provided at checkout.

## Restricted Items
Certain items (e.g., hazardous materials, oversized furniture) may have shipping restrictions and will be noted on the product page.
