# NovaCart — Return & Refund Policy

## Return Eligibility
Most items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery, provided they are unused, in original packaging, and include all accessories/tags.

## Non-Returnable Items
- Gift cards
- Personal care items (opened)
- Perishable goods
- Digital/software products once activated

## How to Initiate a Return
1. Go to "My Orders" in your account.
2. Select the item and click "Request Return."
3. Choose a reason and print the prepaid return label (if eligible).
4. Drop off the package at any authorized carrier location within 7 days of approval.

## Refund Timeline
Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are processed within **5–7 business days** to the original payment method.

## Damaged or Defective Items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, report it within **48 hours** of delivery with photos for a free replacement or full refund — no return shipping required.

## Return Shipping Costs
Return shipping is free for defective/incorrect items. For change-of-mind returns, a $5.99 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund unless the order qualified for free shipping originally.

## Exchanges
NovaCart does not offer direct exchanges. Please return the original item for a refund and place a new order.
