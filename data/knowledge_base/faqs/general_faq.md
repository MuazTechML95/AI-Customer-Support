# NovaCart — General FAQ

## What is NovaCart?
NovaCart is an online e-commerce platform offering electronics, home essentials, and lifestyle products, along with a SaaS-based seller dashboard for small businesses to manage their online stores.

## What are your customer support hours?
Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM (local time). Support is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## How can I contact customer support?
You can reach us via:
- Live chat on our website
- Email: support@novacart.com
- Phone: +1-800-555-0199

## Do you have a mobile app?
Yes, NovaCart is available on both iOS and Android via the App Store and Google Play Store.

## How do I create an account?
Click "Sign Up" on the homepage, enter your email, create a password, and verify your email through the confirmation link.

## Can I shop without an account?
Yes, guest checkout is supported. Creating an account lets you track orders and save addresses.

## What payment methods do you accept?
Credit/debit cards, PayPal, and NovaCart Wallet. See our Payment Policy for details.

## Do you offer international shipping?
Currently NovaCart ships only within the countries listed on our Shipping Policy page.

## How do I track my order?
You'll receive a tracking link via email/SMS once shipped, or check "My Orders" in your account.

## Is my personal information safe?
Yes. NovaCart uses industry-standard encryption and does not sell customer data to third parties.
